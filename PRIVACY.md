# Privacy policy

Glasshouse for Android collects no personal data. It has no accounts,
analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and sends nothing to the developer
or to any third party.

## What stays on the phone

The app keeps a list of the TVs added to it: a name, the TV's address, the
TV's MAC address and Wake-on-LAN setting as the TV reports them, and, where the
TV has one, its dashboard access token. The list is held in the app's private
storage, is excluded from Android backups and from transfers to a new phone,
and is deleted when a TV is removed from the list or the app is uninstalled.

## Network

The app connects only to the TVs in its list, on the local network, to show
each TV's Glasshouse dashboard. That dashboard is served by the Glasshouse
server running on the TV itself. Turning a TV on sends a Wake-on-LAN packet,
which carries only the TV's MAC address, to the local network. Links to other
sites, including those on the About screen, open in the phone's browser.

## Camera

The camera is used only to scan a QR code shown on a TV's dashboard. The image
is read on the phone; nothing from the camera is stored or sent anywhere.
